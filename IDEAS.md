## Risk the panel missed on A: session idle timeout
Realtime closes the session after 5 min without detected USER speech (`session.idle_timeout`), and there is a wall-clock cap (`session.max_duration_reached`). A lecture where the avatar talks 6 min uninterrupted gets cut mid-sentence. Design in: sub-5-min segments or a periodic "with me so far?" that invites a reply; reconnect-and-resume on idle_timeout. Also `max_output_tokens` is clamped at 4096 per response — chunk long lecture turns.
